## 2. Infrastructure Hosting Options & Operating Overhead

### Option A: Lean / Developer Sandbox ($0 – $10 / month)
- **Primary GPU**: Google Colab / Kaggle T4 GPU (16GB VRAM) running `ai_worker/main.py`.
- **Tunneling**: Cloudflare Quick Tunnels (`pycloudflared`).
- **Web/API Server**: Local development machine or $6/mo Hetzner Cloud VPS.
- **Suitability**: Ideal for prototyping, internal testing, and beta validation. Unsuitable for 24/7 commercial multi-user traffic due to Colab session disconnects.

### Option B: Production Serverless On-Demand GPU ($30 – $75 / month base)
- **GPU Architecture**: Serverless GPU containers via **RunPod Serverless** or **Modal Labs** running custom Docker containers with PyTorch, LivePortrait, SDXL-Lightning, and DWPose.
- **Billing Model**: Pay-per-second only during active user generations ($0.0002/sec on L4 GPU). Zero idle cost when no users are generating.
- **Web & Database Server**: $12/month Hetzner CPX31 (4 vCPU, 8GB RAM) running Node.js and SQLite.
- **Object Storage**: Cloudflare R2 (10GB free tier; $0.015/GB thereafter, zero egress fees).

## 3. Commercial Subscription Modeling (Creator SaaS)

Under an AI character & video studio model, pricing is benchmarked against tools like Runway ($15/mo), Pika ($10/mo), and InVideo ($25/mo):

| Plan Tier | Monthly Price (USD) | Monthly Price (INR) | Monthly Video Allowance | Estimated COGS / User | Gross Profit Margin |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Starter Creator** | **$19 / mo** | **₹1,499 / mo** | 20 Videos (15s each) + 5 Persistent Characters | ~$1.80 (₹155) | **90.5%** |
| **Pro Studio** | **$49 / mo** | **₹3,999 / mo** | 75 Videos (15s–45s) + Unlimited Characters + Custom Reference Uploads | ~$7.50 (₹645) | **84.7%** |
| **Agency / Brand** | **$129 / mo** | **₹9,999 / mo** | 250 Videos + Priority GPU Queue + Dedicated Export Watermark Removal | ~$26.00 (₹2,236) | **79.8%** |

### Retry & Failed Generation Buffer
- In generative video, an average of **15%–20% of generations** may be rejected or regenerated by the user due to anatomical artifacts or unexpected pose jitter.
- The unit economics model incorporates a **1.25x compute multiplier** to ensure gross profit margins remain comfortably above 75% even with frequent user retries.
